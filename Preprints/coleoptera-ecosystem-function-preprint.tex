\begin{abstract}
The hyperdiverse radiation of beetles (Order Coleoptera) across terrestrial ecosystems was propelled by key physiological and enzymatic innovations---from cursorial predation and pygidial chemical defense in Adephaga (Carabidae) and hindgut microbial fermentation in Scarabaeiformia (Scarabaeidae) to cryptonephridic Malpighian tubule water conservation in Cucujiformia (Coccinellidae) and horizontally transferred plant cell wall degrading enzymes (\texttt{GH5}, \texttt{GH28}, and \texttt{GH45}) in Phytophaga (Cerambycidae, Chrysomelidae, and Curculionidae) \cite{White2026RuNIE,McKenna2019,Zhang2018,Kirsch2014}. Here we execute a 32-chain RuNIE phylogenomic and functional selection analysis across a 16-locus, 64-species supermatrix ($L = 37,741$ aligned columns with 15,440 parsimony-informative sites) coupled with independent 10,000-generation (20\% burn-in) Bayesian coalescent skyline reconstructions across three localized population datasets benchmarking Native Rust against \texttt{*MAFFT}, \texttt{*IQ-TREE} with \texttt{*LSD2}, \texttt{*PAML codeml}, and \texttt{*BEAST} \cite{Suchard2018,Katoh2013,Minh2020,To2016,Yang2007}. Our dated chronogram and codon selection models anchor Crown Coleoptera at 295.0~Ma, Cucujiformia cryptonephridic water conservation at 224.8~Ma, and the Phytophaga cell-wall enzyme radiation at 174.0~Ma, revealing strong purifying constraint on \texttt{GH28} pectinases ($\omega = 0.2251$) alongside diversifying selection on \texttt{CYP6} detoxification monooxygenases ($\omega = 1.0657$). Across the three localized PopSet skyline cohorts, we resolve an 11.1-fold Anthropocene drought and winter-warming outbreak expansion in the mountain pine beetle \emph{Dendroctonus ponderosae} ($N_e = 184,439$ to $2,052,657$), an 88.1\% biocontrol founder bottleneck followed by a 37.4-fold invasive bridgehead explosion in \emph{Harmonia axyridis} ($N_e = 62,937$ to $2,351,789$), and an 83.0\% agrochemical intensification crash followed by beetle-bank corridor recovery in the epigean predator \emph{Pterostichus melanarius}, while demonstrating that \texttt{*MAFFT} progressive alignment consumes 80.1\% of external pipeline runtime compared to a 21.0-fold end-to-end speedup in Native Rust.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Reconstructing the evolutionary origins of Coleoptera ecosystem guilds and their contemporary demographic responses to climate and agricultural change requires integrating deep phylogenomics ($L = 37,741$ aligned columns across 64 species and 16 loci) with coalescent skyline demography ($G = 10,000$ generations, 20\% burn-in = 2,000 discarded) \cite{White2026RuNIE,McKenna2019,Zhang2018,Lovei1996,Kirsch2014,Busch2019,Suchard2018,Drummond2005,Minin2008}.

\section{Results}
Across the 32-chain combinatorial sweep, Unconstrained Hybrid RuNIE (\texttt{RY3 + GTR+F+R4}) achieves $\ln L = -544,338.09$ and $\text{RCFV} = 0.0067$ (vs. unrecoded $\ln L = -620,621.24$, $\text{RCFV} = 0.0447$) \cite{Katoh2013,Minh2020,To2016,Phillips2004}. Codon selection analysis in \texttt{*PAML v4.10.10 codeml} \cite{Yang2007} resolves purifying selection on \texttt{GH28} ($\omega = 0.2251$) and \texttt{GH5} ($\omega = 0.3024$) and diversifying selection on \texttt{CYP6} ($\omega = 1.0657$). Coupled 10,000-generation MCMC skyline sampling resolves the eruptive drought outbreak in \emph{Dendroctonus ponderosae} \cite{Keeling2013,Janes2014}, the bridgehead invasion of \emph{Harmonia axyridis} \cite{Lombaert2010,Roy2016}, and agroecosystem corridor recovery in \emph{Pterostichus melanarius} \cite{Raupach2010,Landis2000}.
