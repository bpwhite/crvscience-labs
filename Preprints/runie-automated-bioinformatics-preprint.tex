\begin{abstract}
Most bioinformatics workflows stitch together separate command-line programs through intermediate text files, exposing genome-scale studies to silent reading-frame errors, false 100\% statistical certainty under small evolutionary model mismatches, and mislabeled database contaminants such as nuclear pseudogenes. We present \textbf{RuNIE v3.0.0} (\emph{bio-suite v0.4.0}), an open-source (GNU GPL-3.0) native Rust platform that replaces text-file handoffs with shared in-memory data structures, uniting neural Stage 0 \emph{bio-suite-seqnet} database screening, 39 calibrated native Rust engines (40 total), automated evolutionary model gating, blinded SHA-256 sequence-versus-label auditing, and the interactive \emph{runie-align-view} alignment workbench. Across 66 curated validation datasets (\texttt{VAL-SET-001} through \texttt{VAL-SET-066}), over 1,000 negative controls, and 27 verified empirical preprints (\texttt{PREPRINT-01} through \texttt{PREPRINT-27}), RuNIE runs 23.19$\times$ faster end-to-end with 17.3$\times$ less memory (and up to 37.34$\times$ faster on Bayesian coalescent skylines) while cutting compositional bias by 94.2\%, quarantining pseudogenes that otherwise inflate divergence dates by $+142.8\%$, and producing zero topological false positives.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and Failure Modes}
Connecting standalone bioinformatics binaries through shell scripts and plain-text files breaks down at genome scale through silent reading-frame corruption and skipped compositional model checks, frequentist variance collapse ($|Z_{\text{freq}}| \rightarrow \infty$ even under tiny systematic model mismatches), and contaminated public database records compounded by unblinded analysis. RuNIE resolves all three failure modes with shared zero-copy memory structures (\texttt{Arc<AlignmentTensor>}), Stage 0 \emph{bio-suite-seqnet} triage, mandatory Stage 4 \emph{ModelTest} (BIC/AICc/RCFV $\le 0.050$) gating, Dual-Pass SHA-256 blinded verification, interactive residue inspection via \emph{runie-align-view}, and strict separation of empirical preprints from synthetic procedural calibration controls.

\section{Mathematical Formulation and Three Empirical Proofs}
RuNIE optimizes pipeline utility $S(P \mid D)$ via a 4-chain parallel-tempering GA-MCMC controller. Across three empirical proofs on real NCBI RefSeq/GenBank sequences: (1) Stage 4 \emph{ModelTest} + Dayhoff-6 recoding collapses 3rd-codon AT3 non-stationarity by 94.2\% (RCFV $0.3110 \rightarrow 0.0180$) and restores true Ecdysozoa/Holomycota monophyly; (2) Dual-Pass SHA-256 blinded verification flags spiked nuclear NUMT/NUPT pseudogenes ($D_{\text{ctx}} = 6.24$--$6.48$ SD), preventing +142.8\% root-age inflation; and (3) a $2 \times 2 \times 2$ factorial across 83 Hominidae records proves $\Delta \ln L = +1,877.9$ nats epistatic synergy and a 21.3$\times$ reduction in node-age calibration RMSE.

\section{Complete 40-Engine Validation Ledger}
\emph{bio-suite v0.4.0} expands our validated toolkit from 35 to 40 engines: neural Stage 0 \emph{bio-suite-seqnet}, upgraded \#1v4 \emph{mafft-native} (23.41$\times$ faster than \emph{*MAFFT} on ITS spacers), \#36 \emph{na-1d2d3d-tertiary} (7 PDBs, RMSD $0.36$ \AA{}, TM $r = 0.978$), \#37 \emph{htt-somatic-repeat} (0.724 ms standalone, $r = 0.9994$), \#38 \emph{runie-mcmc-skyline} ($578$ ms vs. $21,580$ ms = 37.34$\times$ vs. \emph{*BEAST}), and \#39 \emph{bio-suite-supervisor} (5-tab Win32 GUI + 32-chain loop-back orchestration superseding standalone \#27).

\subsection{Interactive Residue-Level Auditing (\emph{runie-align-view})}
Modeled on \emph{AliView} and built natively in Rust alongside Tool \#38, \emph{runie-align-view} provides smooth 1--24 px/column zooming, six alphabet modes (\texttt{DNA}, \texttt{AA-3BP}, \texttt{AA-1COL}, \texttt{DUAL DNA+AA}, \texttt{RY3}, and \texttt{Dayhoff-6}), NCBI Genetic Codes 1/2/5/11, five residue palettes, and automated headless PNG/SVG snapshots invoked by \texttt{\#39 bio-suite-supervisor} (Figures 3B--3D).
