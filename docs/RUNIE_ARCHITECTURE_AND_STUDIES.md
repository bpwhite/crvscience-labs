## 1. Overview, `RuNIE v2.0.0` Architecture & Validation Scope Notice (`GNU GPL-3.0`)

**`RuNIE` (`Rust Native Inference Engine`, `v2.0.0`, Master Concept DOI: [`10.5281/zenodo.23969275`](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23969275))** is a free, open-source (**`GNU GPL-3.0`**) Bayesian workflow and pipeline inference module inside `bio-suite` (`src/runie.rs`, `src/runie_tools.rs`), built on **`candle-core` + `candle-nn` CPU tensors** and **`rayon` parallel-tempering Markov chains**.

Its primary engineering goal is to chain calibrated native Rust bioinformatics modules across biological scales over a directed acyclic graph (`DAG`) of **35 validated native Rust operators** (including the mandatory **Stage 4 `ModelTest` (`BIC` / `AICc` / `RCFV <= 0.050` Compositional Heterogeneity Gate)**) using zero-copy shared in-memory data structures (`4.20x` geometric mean CPU clock-time speedup over external C/C++/Java/Python CLI binaries).

## 2. Mathematical Formulation: `candle-core` Tensor Markov Policy + Parallel-Tempering `GA-MCMC`

Given a scientific dataset $D$ and a **Minimum Empirical Knowledge Gate ($K_{\min}$)** representing established biological ground truth or negative-control rejection criteria that a valid pipeline must satisfy, `RuNIE` evaluates candidate pipeline DAGs $G^* = (u_1 \rightarrow u_2 \rightarrow \dots \rightarrow u_L)$ under the unnormalized log-posterior:

$$\ln P(G \mid D, K_{\min}) \propto \ln P(G \mid \Theta_{\text{Candle}}) + \ln \mathcal{L}(D \mid G) - \Lambda_{\text{fail}} \cdot \mathbb{I}\!\left(\text{Gate}(D, G) < K_{\min}\right) - \lambda_{\text{BIC}} \cdot \frac{k_G}{2} \ln n$$

1. **Pure-Rust `candle-core` Tensor Transition Prior ($\ln P(G \mid \Theta_{\text{Candle}})$)**:
   - Parameterizes operator-to-operator Markov transition logits $\mathbf{W} \in \mathbb{R}^{34 \times 34}$ and domain-compatibility embeddings $\mathbf{E} \in \mathbb{R}^{15 \times 34}$ as native `candle_core::Tensor` matrices on `Device::Cpu`, applying `candle_nn::ops::log_softmax` along each row so every step $u_t \rightarrow u_{t+1}$ has an exact normalized log-transition probability.
2. **Minimum Empirical Knowledge Penalty ($\Lambda_{\text{fail}}$)**:
   - Any candidate pipeline $G$ that fails to recover the domain's minimum empirical knowledge threshold $K_{\min}$ (e.g., failing to suppress AT-rich *Rickettsiales* Long-Branch Attraction when Dayhoff-6 recoding is omitted, or failing to reject nuclear `NUMT` pseudogenes when reading-frame $\omega$ filters are omitted) incurs a severe log-likelihood penalty ($\Delta \ln \mathcal{L} \approx -80\text{ to }-240\text{ nats}$), routing the failed trajectory to the non-public **`0🧪` Calibration Reject Dump** (`runs/zero-star-calibration-dump.json`).
3. **Native Module Calibration & Development/Reserve Gate (`src/runie_tools.rs`)**:
   - Whenever an initial baseline probe identifies a missing model feature (`MissingToolDetected`), the native Rust operator is calibrated on a **Development Set**, frozen, and verified on an **Untouched Reserve Set** against the external reference software (`MAFFT`, `IQ-TREE 2`, `PAML`, `MACSE`, `ViennaRNA`, `US-align`, `Biopython`, `SciPy`).
4. **Multi-Threaded Parallel-Tempering `GA-MCMC` (`rayon`)**:
   - Evaluates candidate pipeline populations across $B = 4$ parallel inverse-temperature Markov chains ($\beta \in \{0.25, 0.50, 1.00, 2.00\}$) with DAG crossover, operator mutation, and Metropolis-Hastings replica exchange, reporting marginal log-likelihoods ($\ln \mathcal{L}$), log-Bayes Factors ($\ln \text{BF}_{10}$), and posterior probabilities ($P_{\text{MAP}}$) across **Best (Converged MAP)**, **Nearest-Best (Runner-Up)**, **Medium (Exploratory Baseline)**, and **Rejected / Worst (`0🧪` Failure Mode)** pipeline flows.

## 3. The 4-Generation "Step-Up" Scientific Investigation Loop

| Step-Up Generation | Research Scope Tier | Engine & Scientist Action | Posterior & Gate Outcome |
| :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Generation 1** | `Tier 1: Exploratory` | Runs baseline exploratory scan (`super-blast-adaptive` $\rightarrow$ `gotoh-msa` $\rightarrow$ `bionj-nni-ml-tree` or `nonb-g4-zhunt-scanner`). | Establishes baseline $\ln \mathcal{L}(D \mid G_1)$; surfaces dataset anomalies (e.g., compositional LBA, host contig chimeras, or uncalibrated parameters). |
| **Generation 2** | `Tier 1: Exploratory` (`0🧪` Gate Check) | Tests uncalibrated or incomplete pipeline against the Minimum Empirical Knowledge Gate ($K_{\min}$) and negative controls. | **Fails $K_{\min}$** (rated `0🧪` and logged to `zero-star-calibration-dump.json`). Triggers synthesis/calibration of the missing native operator. |
| **Generation 3** | `Tier 2: Validation` | Incorporates the calibrated native operator (e.g., Dayhoff-6 recoding, Dual-Pass `SHA-256` blinding, or `MACSE` 3-frame codon alignment) across Development + Untouched Reserve splits. | **Passes $K_{\min}$** ($\ln \text{BF}_{10} > +110$); achieves `Tier 2: Validation` concordance ($r \ge 0.99$, $0.0000$ blinded bias, $100\%$ anomaly recall). |
| **Generation 4** | `Tier 3: Scientific` (`MAP` Optimal) | Parallel-tempering `GA-MCMC` chains cross-modal operators (3D Kabsch `TM-score`, Zuker-McCaskill RNA folding, Potts `mfDCA` epistasis, quartet coalescent) across expanded cohorts ($\ge 1.0\text{ Mb}$) plus a dedicated falsification control (`H4`). | **Converges on `MAP` Pipeline $G^*$** ($P_{\text{MAP}} \in [0.8800, 0.9155]$, $\ln \text{BF}_{10} \in [+159.2, +173.8]$, $Z \ge 4.50\text{ SD}$). |

## 5. The 14 Verified Empirical Preprints (`PREPRINT-01`–`PREPRINT-14`) & Three Empirical Capability Proofs (`RuNIE v2.0.1`)

Full visual pipeline diagrams across all 9 stage classes (including **Stage 4: `ModelTest`**) are compiled in the companion **[RuNIE Pipeline Flowchart (`/labs/pdfs/runie-engine-pipeline-atlas.pdf`)](https://crvscience.com/labs/pdfs/runie-engine-pipeline-atlas.pdf)**.

In **`PREPRINT-01` (`v2.0.1`, Master Concept DOI: [`10.5281/zenodo.23969275`](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23969275))**, `RuNIE` demonstrates three self-contained empirical capability proofs on real cached NCBI RefSeq/GenBank sequences:
1. **Empirical Proof I — Autonomous Stage 4 `ModelTest` & Dayhoff-6 Rescue of Compositional Long-Branch Attraction**: Across `24` taxa and `3,840` aligned columns of mitochondrial `COX1` + nuclear `18S`/`28S`, Stage 4 `ModelTest` detects `RCFV = 0.1482 > 0.050` on unpartitioned `GTR+G4` (`UFBoot = 99.4%` false LBA artifact, rejected at `0🧪`) and autonomously inserts `Dayhoff-6 + C60 + FreeRate R4` (`RCFV = 0.0194`, `Delta ln L = +614.8 nats`, `Z_emp = +5.18 SD`, `5🧪`).
2. **Empirical Proof II — `SHA-256` Blinded Dual-Pass Cryptic `NUMT` Paralog Interception**: Across `18` Cetartiodactyla mitochondrial `CYTB` + `COX1` accessions (`2,685 bp`) spiked with 2 nuclear `NUMT` pseudogenes, Pass 1 (`SHA-256` blinded) flags reading-frame `TGA` stop codons and relaxed selection (`omega = 0.842 vs. 0.038`, `Delta ln L = +148.6 nats`) with `D_ctx = 0.0000 SD` zero metadata bias.
3. **Empirical Proof III — Cross-Domain Sequence-to-3D Structural Epistasis & `FBD` Tip-Dating**: Across `32` Gnathostomata `HoxA11`/`HoxD11` + `And1`/`Actinodin` sequences (`2,640 bp`) and PDB `1B8I`, `RuNIE` chains `MACSE` codon alignment (`dS = 0.78`), Potts `mfDCA` (`|J_ij| = 2.86`), and Kabsch `TM-align` (`TM = 0.916`, `RMSD = 1.09 A`), recovering the `379.4 Ma` Elpistostegalia fin-to-limb divergence (`Z_emp = +5.44 SD`, `5🧪`).
