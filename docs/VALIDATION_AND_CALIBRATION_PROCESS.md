## 1. The 3-Tier Research Scope System (`Tier 1`, `Tier 2`, `Tier 3`)

Every study in CRV Science Labs is classified into one of three explicit **Research Scope Tiers** that define both the scientific scope and the dataset provenance policy:

| Scope Tier | Badge | Dataset Policy & Scientific Objective |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Tier 1: Exploratory** | `🧭 Tier 1: Exploratory` | Seeks out and explores a mix of newly acquired, classic reference, or previously unseen datasets, or applies an existing validation cohort in a new analytical way during tool calibration sweeps (`*-compare`). |
| **Tier 2: Validation** | `🎯 Tier 2: Validation` | Uses curated validation datasets (`VAL-SET-001` through `VAL-SET-025`) partitioned into strict **Development (`Dev` — parameter tuning)** and **Untouched Reserve (`Res` — frozen parameter verification)** splits, with primary literature replication and optional **Dual-Pass Blinded `SHA-256` Validation**. |
| **Tier 3: Scientific** | `🔬 Tier 3: Scientific` | Combines established validation cohorts with newly expanded empirical datasets ($\ge 1.0\text{ Mb}$) across 5–8 chained `RuNIE` modules to test multiple mechanistic hypotheses (`H1`–`H3`) alongside a dedicated **Hypothesis Falsification Control (`H4`)**, producing inline vector `.svg` Graphical Reports and 3D PDB structure bundles. |

## 2. Scope vs. Depth: `1🧪`–`5🧪` Beaker Signal Rating Gate (`B_prior` $\rightarrow$ `B_posterior`)

We separate **Research Scope (`Tier 1`–`3`)** from **Analytical Depth & Empirical Signal Strength (`0🧪`–`5🧪`)**. When designing an experiment targeting a given depth prior ($B_{\text{prior}}$), the final published rating is always adjusted to the honest **Empirical Posterior ($B_{\text{posterior}}$)** based on observed effect sizes ($Z$-scores), sample scale, and negative-control boundaries:

| Beaker Rating | Label | Quantitative Gate & Empirical Criteria |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **`0🧪`** *(Excluded)* | **Calibration Reject / Null Noise** | Collapsed barcoding gap ($\le 0.0$), bootstrap $< 70\%$, sliding-window $Z < 2.50\text{ SD}$ without mobile elements, single-copy housekeeping operons without BGC synthases, or shuffled/neutral decoys ($JSD < 0.05\text{ bits}$). **Excluded from standalone publication** and logged to `runs/zero-star-calibration-dump.json`. |
| **`🧪` (`1/5`)** | **Baseline / Review Note** | Small test datasets, single-locus benchmarks ($N = 1$ contig), intragenomic paralog baselines, known reference rediscoveries (`MIBiG` Jaccard $\ge 0.80$), or falsified Tier 3 hypothesis strata (`H4` negative controls). |
| **`🧪🧪` (`2/5`)** | **Technical Validation** | Multi-clade or multi-locus ($\ge 5$ phased loci / 12 Dev+Reserve cohorts) reproducible benchmark confirming established biological or algorithmic parameters ($r \ge 0.99$, `100%` regime concordance). |
| **`🧪🧪🧪` (`3/5`)** | **Comparative Synthesis** | Cross-clade ($\ge 25$ taxa) or multi-locus ($\ge 250\text{ kb}$) synthesis uncovering structured quantitative trade-offs or failure boundaries across engines ($Z \ge 3.00\text{ SD}$). |
| **`🧪🧪🧪🧪` (`4/5`)** | **Strong Emergent Signal** | Large, well-powered cohorts ($\ge 250\text{ kb}$ to $800\text{ kb}$, $\ge 3$ chained pipelines, $Z \ge 3.50\text{ SD}$) demonstrating multi-modal concordance across Development and Untouched Reserve partitions. |
| **`🧪🧪🧪🧪🧪` (`5/5`)** | **Significant Biological Signal** | Flagship multi-stage syntheses ($\ge 1.0\text{ Mb}$ / $\ge 50$ taxa or loci, $\ge 4$ chained `RuNIE` engines, positive-stratum $Z \ge 4.80\text{ SD}$ and all-stratum $Z \ge 4.50\text{ SD}$ including falsified negative controls). |

## 3. Dual-Pass Blinded `SHA-256` Validation & Repository Anomaly Detection (`D_ctx`)

To prove that `RuNIE` pipelines recover biological signal from sequence and structural physics rather than metadata priors, Tier 2 Validation supports **Dual-Pass Blinded Mode** ([`#73 🚩`](https://crvscience.com/labs/discoveries/popgen-tier2-blind-validate), [`#83 🚩`](https://crvscience.com/labs/discoveries/heimdall-tier2-blind-validate)):

1. **Pass 1 (Blinded De-Identified Extrapolation)**: All species names, gene symbols, accession headers, and subpopulation labels are stripped and replaced with opaque `SHA-256` tokens (`BLIND-COHORT-*`). `RuNIE` extrapolates the molecular biotype, evolutionary regime, and signal strength ($Z_{\text{blind}}$) strictly from raw sequence/structure tensors (2nd-codon entropy $H_{\text{pos2}}$, tetranucleotide $D_{KL}$, Dayhoff-6 `RCFV`, 3D Kabsch `TM-score`).
2. **Pass 2 (Context-Aware Unblinding & Discordance Audit)**: Metadata labels are re-attached to compute the **Label-vs-Sequence Discordance Score**:
   $$D_{\text{ctx}} = |Z_{\text{blind}} - Z_{\text{ctx}}|$$
   - When $D_{\text{ctx}} = 0.0000\text{ SD}$, the blinded pass confirms zero confirmation bias.
   - When $D_{\text{ctx}} \ge 2.50\text{ SD}$, the audit automatically flags **published repository context anomalies**—such as nuclear `NUMT` pseudogenes mislabelled as mitochondrial `COX1`, *Acanthamoeba* or arthropod host contigs misbinned into viral/bacterial MAGs, 5′/3′ rRNA PCR chimeras, or lateral gene transfers (`LGT`).

## 4. Genomic Marker Diagnostic Power & Taxonomic Resolution Horizons

All systematics and barcoding pipelines evaluate markers against their empirical resolution window (`labs/releases/marker-diagnostic-power-matrix.json`):

| Genomic Marker | Optimal Diagnostic Window | Empirical Failure Boundary (`0🧪`/`1🧪` Trap) | Corrective Assay Rescue Protocol |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Prokaryotic `16S rRNA` (`rrsA`–`rrsH`)** | Phylum $\rightarrow$ Genus ($H_{\text{stem}} = 0.49\text{ bits}$, $H_{\text{loop}} = 1.68\text{ bits}$) | Collapses in enteric sister species (*E. coli* intragenomic `rrsA`–`rrsH` divergence `0.0091` $>$ inter-species distance vs. *Salmonella* `0.0078`, gap $= -0.0013$). | Long-read `rrnA`–`rrnH` operon phasing + single-copy `rpoB`/`gyrB` ([`#29`](https://crvscience.com/labs/discoveries/bacterial-rpob-gyrb-vs-16s)) or whole-contig 4-mer $D_{KL}$. |
| **Prokaryotic `23S rRNA` (`Domain V PTC`)** | Deep clades & macrolide/linezolid resistance | Isolated sub-clonal SNV calls fail without epistatic context. | Potts compensatory coupling verification ($\|J_{2058,2611}\| = 2.642$, escape barrier $\Delta E = +14.7\text{ kcal/mol}$, [`#4`](https://crvscience.com/labs/discoveries/ribosomal-rna-23s-ptc-epistasis)). |
| **Eukaryotic Nuclear `18S rRNA` (`RN18S1`)** | Supergroup $\rightarrow$ Phylum ($94.2\%$ CBC conservation) | Collapses within fungal and vertebrate genera (*Mus* vs. *Rattus* $99.89\%$ identical across $1,870\text{ nt}$, $d_{\text{K2P}} = 0.0011$). | Use `18S` stems for universal primer binding; switch to `5.8S`-anchored `ITS1–ITS2` or `COX1` + `MT-RNR2` for species. |
| **Eukaryotic `ITS1–5.8S–ITS2` Cistron** | Fungal & plant species ($19.48\times$ gap, $98.4\%$ bootstrap when `5.8S`-anchored) | Unanchored `ITS1` collapses across families ($48.6\%$ gaps, $\text{Ti/Tv} = 0.54$, $31\%$ bootstrap). | Anchor alignment to invariant $158\text{ bp}$ `5.8S` ($H = 0.34\text{ bits}$) + `ITS2` Helix III Compensatory Base Changes ([`#3`](https://crvscience.com/labs/discoveries/fungal-its-58s-cbc-barcoding)). |
| **Mitochondrial `COX1` + `MT-RNR2` (`16S mtDNA`)** | Metazoan species ($16.4\times–22.1\times$ gap; $H_{\text{pos2}} = 0.000\text{ bits}$, $\omega = 0.031$) | Truncated $<200\text{ bp}$ mini-barcodes collapse (gap $= -0.014$); nuclear `NUMT` paralogs ($\omega = 0.89$) mimic cryptic species. | Pair full $658\text{ bp}$ `COX1` + `MT-RNR2` ($5.82\times$ coupled gap), filter `NUMTs` via reading-frame/$\omega < 0.10$ ([`#30`](https://crvscience.com/labs/discoveries/vertebrate-cox1-mtrnr2-numt-filter)). |
| **Deep Phylogenomic `RP16` / `RP56` + `RNAP`** | Phylum $\rightarrow$ Domain (Tree of Life, Asgard, Proto-Mitochondria) | Unrecoded 20-state AA matrices succumb to thermophilic (`GARP`) or AT-rich (`FYMINK`) Long-Branch Attraction (`RCFV > 0.12`). | Recode into **Dayhoff-6 ($k = 6$)** or **SR4 ($k = 4$)** physicochemical states + `LG+C60+F+G4` ([`#66`](https://crvscience.com/labs/discoveries/rp16-dayhoff-recoding-iqtree), [`#84 🚩`](https://crvscience.com/labs/discoveries/heimdall-eukaryogenesis-tier3), [`#86 🚩`](https://crvscience.com/labs/discoveries/mito-endosymbiosis-tier3)). |
